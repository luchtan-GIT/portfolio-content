
I have composed for bells, for tesla coils, for grallas, for voice, with books, for computers, for carillons, for dancers, for ghosts.  I am currently composing for trains.  
I have written music in traditional notation, contemporary notation, midi notation, without notation,I have carved it ona roll of vellum with an exacto knife,and painted it on a wall as a mural.  
I have given performances of my compositions and installations in Barcelona and Black Mountain.  I have built instruments out of rusty pianos and long strings, played melodies on bipolar tesla coils.
